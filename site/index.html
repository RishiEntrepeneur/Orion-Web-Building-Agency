What I build

Four things, done properly.

Not a menu of everything. These are the things I do well, and I would rather tell you when something is outside them than take the work and learn on your money.

01

Business sites that convert

Five to ten pages that load instantly, read clearly on a phone, and put the thing you want people to do in front of them. Built to be found: proper headings, real metadata, structured data where it earns its place.

02

Landing pages that earn their spend

One page, one job. If you are paying for traffic, the page it lands on should be the fastest and clearest thing in the funnel — not the slowest.

03

Rescue work

An existing site that is slow, broken on mobile, or unusable with a keyboard. I measure it first, tell you what is actually wrong, and quote to fix the things that matter rather than everything at once.

04

Motion, where it helps

Scroll-driven sequences and real-time graphics when the product needs showing rather than describing. Used sparingly, and never at the cost of the page being fast.

How it works

Four steps, and you always know where you are.

  1. Day 0You send a brief

    A paragraph is enough. What the business does, who it is for, and what you want a visitor to do. I reply the same working day with a fixed price and a date.

  2. Day 1–2Structure and copy

    Pages, sections and the words, agreed before a pixel is designed. This is the step most people skip and most regret skipping.

  3. Day 3–6Build

    Written by hand. You get a live link on day three and it updates as I work, so nothing is a surprise at the end.

  4. Day 7Launch and handover

    Your domain, your hosting account, your code. I show you how to edit the things you will want to change, and you own all of it.
