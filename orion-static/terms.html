Terms

Terms for using this website. The terms for working together are a separate document, agreed in writing before anything begins.

Last updated 25 August 2026 England & Wales Plain English

Who you are dealing with

Orion is one person in England, not a company. It is not registered at Companies House, it has no office and it has no employees. Everything is agreed in writing before work starts, and that written agreement is the contract between us.

Payment and contracting are handled by an adult in my family, whose name appears on the invoice and on the agreement. That is who you are contracting with, and it is deliberate: it means the agreement is with somebody who can be held to it. The work itself is mine.

Using this site

You are welcome to read it, quote it with attribution, link to it and take ideas from it. Please do not present its content as your own, republish it wholesale, or copy the design to pass off as somebody else's work.

The claims on this site

Every figure published here is measured, not estimated, and the scripts that measure them are part of the source. Where a number moves as the site changes, it is re-measured rather than left standing. If you find one that is wrong, tell me and it gets fixed.

There is no client work shown on this site, because there is none to show yet. The teardown describes this website and nothing else.

Prices

The three package prices on the packages page are real and are what I charge, in pounds sterling. Each is a one-off build fee plus a monthly fee, and the monthly fee covers hosting and the ongoing work listed under that package — nothing else. A domain name is bought in your own name and billed by whoever you buy it from, so it is not part of those figures. Cancelling the monthly does not take the site away from you; it ends the hosting and the updates. A published price is not a binding offer — what binds is the written agreement we both accept before work starts.

Accuracy

I keep this site correct to the best of my ability. The build notes describe how I solved a problem at the time of writing; they are not advice for your situation, and browsers change. Test things yourself before relying on them.

Links out

Where this site links somewhere else, it is because I found it useful. I do not control those sites and I am not responsible for their content or their privacy practices.

For work I build for you, everything transfers to you outright on final payment — source, notes and the scripts that check it. That is a term of every job, not a negotiation.
