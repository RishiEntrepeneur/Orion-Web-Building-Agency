    02 After you pay Four steps

    What happens next

    1. 01 A receipt, straight away Emailed by Stripe, not by me, so it arrives whether or not I am at a computer. Keep it — it is your proof of payment.
    2. 02 I confirm the scope in writing Within a day: what I am building, what I need from you, and when it will be live. If anything in it surprises you, say so before I start rather than after.
    3. 03 You see it early Direction arrives as a real page in a real browser, not a picture of one. Nothing is a surprise at the end.
    4. 04 It goes live, and it is yours Source, notes and the checking scripts, handed over. The monthly starts the month after it is live, not the day you paid.

    Paying safely

    Your card never touches this site

    The pay button hands you to Stripe, who take the card details on their own page. This site has no server, stores nothing and never sees a card number — which is deliberate, and the reason I have not built a card form of my own. Anyone who asks you to type card details into a small business's own website is doing it wrong.

    You will see Stripe's domain in the address bar before you type anything. If you do not, close the tab and tell me.

    Payments and contracts are handled by an adult in my family. Stripe requires the account holder to be an adult, and it means the written agreement is with somebody who can be held to it — which matters to you rather more than it does to me. The work is mine; the paperwork is theirs, and their name is on the invoice you will receive.

    Refunds: if I have not started, all of it, no argument. If I have, the fair share of what is left. That is in the written agreement before you pay, not discovered afterwards.
