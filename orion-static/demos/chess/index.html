Thursday teaching game

The Italian, four moves in

Somebody asks how to start almost every week, so here is the answer we usually give. Step through it. White takes the centre, brings out a knight, then a bishop pointed straight at the weakest square on Black's side of the board.

You do not need to memorise it. You need to notice what each piece is doing, which is the entire game in one sentence.

About

Fifty-one years of Thursdays

The club started in 1974 in the back room of the Fox & Hounds, moved to the village hall when the pub stopped doing food, and has met every Thursday since apart from three weeks of snow in 2010 and the obvious gap in 2020.

We run two teams in the county league, a summer rapidplay ladder that nobody takes seriously, and a junior hour before the main session on the first Thursday of the month. Two of our juniors are now stronger than most of the adults, which is exactly how it should go.

If you have not played since school, you are the person we are most pleased to see. Come and lose a few games. Everyone here did.
