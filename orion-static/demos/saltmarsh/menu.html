Yesterday's card

The card, as it stood

Tonight's is written at four. This is what we cooked yesterday, which is the closest anyone can honestly get to it.

  • Opened to order. The vinegar is our own, from bladderwrack picked off the point.

  • Three days in salt and sugar, sliced thin. Pollock because it was what came in.

  • Over embers, rested twenty minutes, carved at the pass. Garlic from the lane.

  • Whatever size the boat brought. We will tell you the weight before you commit.

  • Cut on the falling tide, cooked for ninety seconds, not a minute more.

  • Six hours in the oven that was already on. The barley does the rest.

  • The nettle of the shoreline. Better than spinach and nobody believes us.

  • Honey from three miles inland, taken further than is sensible.

  • Picked along the sea wall in September and frozen for the rest of the year.

  • Ask. It changes, and whoever is on the pass will have a view.

Nothing on the card under that heading yesterday.

Everything is cooked over wood in the room. If you cannot eat something, tell us when you book rather than when you arrive, and we will cook you the same number of courses rather than a plate of vegetables.
