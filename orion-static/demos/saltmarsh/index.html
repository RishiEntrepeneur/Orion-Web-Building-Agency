01 Tonight, probably

The card is written
at four o'clock.

Four courses, one sitting, and a version of it for anyone who does not eat what came off the boat. If you tell us in advance we will cook for you properly rather than apologetically.

See the whole card →

  • Opened to order. The vinegar is our own, from bladderwrack picked off the point.

  • Three days in salt and sugar, sliced thin. Pollock because it was what came in.

  • Over embers, rested twenty minutes, carved at the pass. Garlic from the lane.

  • Whatever size the boat brought. We will tell you the weight before you commit.

  • Cut on the falling tide, cooked for ninety seconds, not a minute more.

02 · The room

Twenty-two seats
and one fire.

It was a chandlery, then a garage, then nothing for eleven years. The floor is the floor it always was. Everything is cooked in the room you are sitting in, which means it is loud, and warm, and you will smell of woodsmoke on the way home.
