The Orion Method

Four steps, no drift.

A fixed pipeline is what makes a fixed deadline credible. You always know which stage you are in and what comes out of it.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Strategy

    We establish what the site has to achieve commercially before a single pixel is drawn, and write it down as the definition of done.

    Output — scope & success criteria

  2. 02

    Architecture & Design

    Information architecture, interface design and the technical plan are produced together, so the design is never something engineering has to argue with.

    Output — design system & tech spec

  3. 03

    Development & Optimisation

    Built against the performance and accessibility budget from day one, with the measurement running continuously rather than as a report at the end.

    Output — staged build & metrics

  4. 04

    Deployment & Launch

    Domain, analytics, monitoring and handover. You receive every source file and the pipeline that builds it.

    Output — live site & full handover
