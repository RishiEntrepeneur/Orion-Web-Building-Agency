02 The Orion method Four steps

The Orion Method

01 02 03 04
Step 01 — Week 1

Discovery
& strategy

We start with the commercial problem, not the moodboard. Stakeholder interviews, analytics forensics, competitor teardown — then a written position we both sign off before a pixel moves.

  • Audience & intent mapping
  • Analytics and funnel forensics
  • Written creative position
Step 02 — Weeks 2–3

Architecture
& design

Information architecture, then art direction. You see a real, interactive design system in the browser — type scale, motion rules, states and edge cases — not a flat picture of a homepage.

  • Sitemap and content model
  • Art direction and type system
  • Interactive component library
Step 03 — Weeks 4–6

Development
& optimisation

Built on a modern framework with a performance budget enforced in CI. Accessibility is checked continuously, not audited at the end when it is expensive to fix.

  • Component build and CMS wiring
  • Performance budget in CI
  • WCAG 2.2 AA verification
Step 04 — Week 7

Deployment
& launch

Staged rollout, redirect maps, analytics and monitoring live from hour one. You get the repository, the documentation and a team that answers the phone afterwards.

  • Staged release and redirect map
  • Monitoring and alerting
  • Handover, docs and training
