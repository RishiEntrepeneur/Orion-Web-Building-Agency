01 Who is behind this Exactly one person

Twelve, and not waiting

I am twelve years old. I live in England, my family is Indian, and I taught myself to build websites because the ones I was shown all looked like each other.

Everything on this site — the type system, the motion, the software 3D in the exploded view, the sound engine, the build script that generates the pages — I wrote. There is no team behind the word we, so I do not use it.

What I have instead of a client list is this website, taken apart in public. If you want to know whether I can build something well, read how this one is put together and judge from that.

02 What I think Five positions

Opinions I will argue for

  1. A budget is a refusal

    A performance target everyone agrees with is a wish. A budget that fails the build is a decision. I use the second kind. I wrote about why.

  2. Accessibility is a build practice

    Checked while the thing is being made, not audited at the end when it is expensive to fix. Every colour pairing on this site had its contrast ratio computed, not eyeballed.

  3. Motion needs a reason and a budget

    Effects added in the final week look like effects added in the final week. Motion here is a system on a frame budget, and every behaviour has a reduced-motion answer before it ships.

  4. Understand it or do not ship it

    Reaching for a library you have not read is how a site ends up with problems nobody on it can fix. This one has no dependencies, so there is nothing in it I cannot explain.

  5. Say the true thing

    This site used to claim a team, an office and six clients. None of that was real, so it is gone. Every number still on these pages was measured, and I will show you how.

03 How working together goes Plainly

What being twelve changes

It changes some things and not others. It does not change how the code is written, whether the contrast passes, or whether the page is fast on a bad connection — those are decisions, and I make them carefully.

It does mean I work around school, so I take on few projects and I will tell you honestly whether I can fit yours. It also means anything commercial goes through my parents, which I would rather say here than surprise you with later.

If I am the wrong person for what you need, I will say so early. That costs me the project and saves you the wait.
