Privacy

The short version: this website collects nothing about you. The longer version is below, and it is short too.

Last updated 25 August 2026 UK GDPR & DPA 2018 No cookies, no analytics

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no embedded third-party scripts and no fonts loaded from someone else's server. Every asset on this page is served from our own domain, which means no third party learns that you visited.

The only thing stored in your browser is a single preference, under the key orion-sound, recording whether you switched interface sound on. It never leaves your device and we never see it. Clearing your site data removes it.

The contact form

The form on our contact page has no server behind it. It validates what you type in your own browser, assembles it into a plain-text brief, and hands that to your email client as a pre-filled draft. Nothing is transmitted anywhere until you press send yourself, in your own mail application.

Once you do send it, we hold your email like any other email: to reply to you and, if we work together, to run the project.

What we do with an enquiry

  • We read it and reply, usually within one business day
  • We keep the thread while there is an active conversation
  • If we do not end up working together, we delete it within twelve months
  • We never add you to a mailing list, a nurture sequence or a CRM you did not ask to be in
  • We never sell, rent or share your details with anyone

Our lawful basis for holding an enquiry is legitimate interest: you contacted a business expecting a reply. For clients, it is performance of a contract.

Where it is held

Email is hosted in the European Economic Area. Project files for active clients are held in the UK and the EEA. We do not transfer personal data outside those regions without an appropriate safeguard in place.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to us holding it. Write to studio@orion.build and we will action it within thirty days. There is no form to fill in and you do not need to explain why.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes materially we will update the date at the top of the page. There is no version history because there has only ever been one version.

Who we are

Orion Studio Ltd, registered in England & Wales. Data protection enquiries: studio@orion.build.
