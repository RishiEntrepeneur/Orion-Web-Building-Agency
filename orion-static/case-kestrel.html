Manufacturing · Precision parts

Kestrel Manufacturing

Quotes took nine days and lived in PDF attachments. We built a configurator that lets a buyer set tolerances and finishes in 3D, prices it against the ERP, and returns a formal quote in about two hours.

2025 Project · 16 weeks Sample project
Manufacturing

The brief

What we were asked to solve

Kestrel machine bespoke parts. Winning work meant quoting it, and quoting it meant a buyer emailing a drawing, an estimator opening it three days later, a phone call to clarify tolerances, and a PDF nine days after the first contact.

They were not losing on price. They were losing on the calendar — to competitors who answered the same day.

01 What we did 05 workstreams

What we did

  1. 01 3D configurator A WebGL part configurator where tolerance, finish and material are set against a live model, so the buyer can see what they are specifying.
  2. 02 Live pricing Configuration priced against the ERP in real time, including material cost and machine time.
  3. 03 Quote pipeline A formal, versioned quote generated and issued without an estimator re-keying anything.
  4. 04 Escalation path Anything the rules cannot price is routed to a human with the configuration attached, rather than rejected.
  5. 05 Device tiering The configurator degrades to a 2D specification form on hardware that cannot run it, because buyers use the machines they have.
