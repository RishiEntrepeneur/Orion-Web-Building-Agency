        02
        What happens next
        
        Four steps
      

      

        No forms into the void
      


      

        

          01
          
I read it myself

          
Not a router, not an autoresponder, not a chatbot. There is only me.

        

        

          02
          
You get a real answer

          
Either a first read on the problem, or an honest no. I would rather say no early than disappear.

        

        

          03
          
We talk it through

          
A short call or a thread, to test whether the problem is what it looks like. No deck.

        

        

          04
          
It goes in writing

          
Scope, timescale and price written down and agreed by both of us before anything starts.

        

      

    

  


  

      

    

      

        

          03
          Before you write
        

        
What helps me answer

        

          
The goal, roughly when you need it live, and what is wrong with what you have now. Three sentences is plenty — I would rather have a short honest message than a long careful one.

          
If you are not sure a website is even the answer, say that too. Sometimes it is not, and telling you so costs me nothing.

          Questions about the form
