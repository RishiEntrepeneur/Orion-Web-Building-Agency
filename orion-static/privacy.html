Orion/Privacy

Privacy

The short version: this website collects nothing about you. The longer version is below, and it is short too.

Last updated 25 August 2026 UK GDPR & DPA 2018 No cookies, no analytics

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no embedded third-party scripts and no fonts loaded from someone else's server. Every asset on this page comes from this domain, which means no third party learns that you visited.

The only thing stored in your browser is a single preference, under the key orion-sound, recording whether you switched interface sound on. It never leaves your device and I never see it. Clearing your site data removes it.

The contact form

The form on the contact page has no server behind it. It checks what you type in your own browser, assembles it into a plain-text brief, and shows it to you. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. You decide whether to send it, and how.

Once you do send me a message, I hold it the way anyone holds an email: to read it and reply.

What happens to a message

  • I read it and reply, usually within a few days
  • I keep the thread while there is a conversation going on
  • If we do not end up working together, I delete it within twelve months
  • You are never added to a mailing list, a sequence or a CRM
  • Your details are never sold, rented or passed to anyone else

The lawful basis for holding a message is legitimate interest: you wrote to me expecting a reply. If we work together, it becomes performance of a contract.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of anything I hold about you, ask me to correct it, ask me to delete it, or object to me holding it at all. Ask through the contact form and it will be actioned within thirty days. There is nothing to fill in and you do not have to explain why.

If you are not satisfied with how it is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the date at the top of the page changes with it. There is no version history because there has only ever been one version.

Who runs this site

Orion is one person in England. It is not a registered company, and nothing on this site should be read as claiming otherwise. Questions about privacy go through the contact form.

Nothing to opt out of
